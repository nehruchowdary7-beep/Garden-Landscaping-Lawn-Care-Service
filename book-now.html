What Happens On Your Scheduled Service Day

From automated arrival notifications to spotless cleanup, our three-step protocol ensures complete peace of mind.

01

Arrival & Perimeter Check

Our crew arrives in full uniform, checks gate latch security, clears dog toys or lawn hoses, and inspects grass height before starting.

02

Precision Cut & Clean Edging

Commercial razor-sharp mulching blades slice grass cleanly, followed by surgical 90-degree trimming along fences, flower beds, and walks.

03

Blow-Off & Secure Exit

All clippings are cleared off patios, driveways, and decks. We close and latch all gates securely and send a real-time completion summary.
